# CelLink

> CelLink (CelLink Technologies / CelLink Corporation) manufactures large-format, high-conductance
> flexible circuits that consolidate power, data, and sensing into thin, lightweight layers,
> replacing traditional wire harnesses and busbars. Products: Battery Pack Interconnect, Flex
> Harness, and Data Center power delivery. Markets: automotive, aerospace, industrial, data center,
> and grid storage. Headquartered in San Carlos, California, with sites in Georgetown, Texas;
> Wixom, Michigan; Hamburg, Germany; and Shanghai, China. CelLink circuits have been in
> electric-vehicle production since 2020.

## Notes

- CelLink is a hardware manufacturer. As of 2026-07-20 it publishes no public developer portal,
  API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI specification, SDK, CLI, or MCP server.
- The former domain cellinkcorp.com no longer resolves (NXDOMAIN). The historical redirect chain
  cellinkcorp.com -> cellinkcircuits.com -> cellinktechnologies.com resolves to the live site.
- No /llms.txt, /.well-known/security.txt, /.well-known/api-catalog, /.well-known/ai-plugin.json,
  /.well-known/openid-configuration, or /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server is published
  (all returned HTTP 404 on 2026-07-20).
